Your first 15 minutesdo this bit on the phone you actually use
12 min
Start your family
  • Tap "Start free", then sign in with your email or Google. There is no password.
  • Pick your family web address, something like /t/khan-family. It is yours and nobody else can see it.
25 min
Add your people
  • Add each child with a name, an avatar and a 4-digit PIN. Kids need no email.
  • Invite the other grown-up by email. Grandparents can be added too.
33 min
Decide what a sticker is worth
  • Kids earn stickers for good habits. You set what one sticker is worth in real money, for the whole family or per child.
  • This is the bit that turns chores into pocket money and money lessons.
45 min
Pick two or three habits per child, then hand the phone over
  • Start small. Teeth, homework, tidy room. You can always add more later.
  • Let each child tap their own avatar and type their PIN. Watch what they do without helping them. That first minute tells us a lot.

Week onejust live in it

Do not go hunting for things to test. Run your normal week and notice where the app helps and where it gets in the way.

Youthe admin
The daily driver
  • Plan the week's meals and put the real school and club dates in the calendar.
  • Put two or three real jobs on the Tasks list and give them to someone.
  • Post one thing on the Noticeboard that the whole house needs to know.

Notice: anything that takes too many taps between school runs.

Your partnerinvited adult
The second grown-up
  • Accept the invite on their own phone and use it for a few days.
  • Both of you open the same list at the same time and see if it stays in step.

Notice: anything they can reach that only you should control.

Your kidsPIN sign-in
Their own world
  • Let them tick their own habits each day and watch their stickers build up.
  • Send them into Learn: maths, logic, world flags, drawing and a reading log.
  • Let them ask for something from the reward shop and approve it yourself.

Notice: anything a child finds confusing, boring or babyish.

Week twothe money week
The moneyclose a week
Where the real value lives
  • Close a week with each child: claim, cash out, save or invest their stickers. Check the maths matches what you would have handed over in coins.
  • Invest some stickers into a habit that grows them, and talk about why.
  • Add the family calendar to your phone's own calendar with the private link.

The big question: did any of this change a conversation at home?

Stuck?quick fixes
The three things that trip people up
  • No sign-in email? Check your spam folder. It comes from noreply@fhapp.co, and the link only works for a short while, so ask for a fresh one if it has been sitting.
  • Forgot a child's PIN? You can reset it yourself on the family members screen.
  • Screen looking odd? Refresh the page first, then sign out and back in.
