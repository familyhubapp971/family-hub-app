The problem

Family life is fragmented across apps, messages and memory.

Mental load

Meals, calendars, school notices and plans live in different places, making coordination harder for everyone.

Structure

Parents repeat reminders. Children struggle to own routines, responsibility and money habits.

Connection

Families can be present in the same room while missing what matters in each other's day.

The cost is more nagging, more coordination and less meaningful time together.
Problem2 / 12
The big idea

What if your family ran on rituals, not reminders?

Family Hub is one private digital home for shared plans, positive habits, early money learning and daily connection.

Solution and value proposition3 / 12
How it works

One weekly rhythm connects the whole product.

Plan

Families share meals, calendars, notices and tasks.

Do

Children complete habits, build streaks and earn stickers.

Learn

Stickers become pocket money, saving choices and rewards.

Connect

Journals, gratitude and learning open better conversations.

No child email. No child phone. No debit card required. Built for families from age four.
