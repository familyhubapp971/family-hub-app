Vision and Mission

Our vision

Every family, one team.
No one carrying it alone.

Our mission

We bring every kind of family onto the same page, and lift the mental load off the people who hold it all together.

Our promise in three words: Same page. Lighter load.

What Family Hub does

An app that achieves three things:

  • 1Same page Helps reduce the mental load on any one parent by creating visibility that keeps the whole family on the same page.
  • 2Habits and money Helps children build new habits, and turns the rewards they earn into lessons in financial literacy.
  • 3Feelings Tracks children's daily emotional wellbeing to reveal patterns over time.

What we believe

  • 1Every family belongs There is no "normal" family. Just yours.
  • 2Lighter, never louder If it adds work, we don't build it.
  • 3One shared plan Everyone knows. Nobody chases.
  • 4Kids are on the team Their own jobs. Their own wins.
  • 5Your family, your space Private by default. No feed, no audience.
