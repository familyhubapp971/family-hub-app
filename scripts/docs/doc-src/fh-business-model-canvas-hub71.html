Hub71 edition · Demand side

Business Model Canvas

One private family space that shares the mental load, builds structure through rituals and keeps families in each other's worlds.

01 · Customer segments

Households and the organisations that serve them

  • Core: tech-comfortable parents with children aged four and above who juggle multiple apps.
  • Underserved: blended, multigenerational, multicultural and large households.
  • Institutional: schools, clubs, co-ops and family organisations serving 20 to 500+ families.
  • Launch market: UAE first, GCC next, then culturally adaptable global expansion.
02 · Value propositions

Less chaos. More capable children. More family connection.

  • Shared structure: meals, calendars, notices and tasks in one private home view.
  • Positive routines: habits become stickers, streaks and rewards children can own.
  • Early money learning: earn, save and invest without requiring a debit card.
  • Daily connection: journals, gratitude and learning open better conversations.
  • Institutional reach: one secure multi-tenant platform that partners can brand.
03 · Channels

Direct acquisition plus institutional distribution

  • Parent referrals, community content and targeted search.
  • Schools, clubs and family organisations as trusted distribution partners.
  • Hub71 introductions to ECA, ADEK, Family Care Authority and school groups.
  • Web app at launch; native mobile distribution as adoption grows.
04 · Customer relationships

Self-serve for families, hands-on for partners

  • Fast guided onboarding with no child email, phone or bank card required.
  • Weekly prompts, progress recaps and reward-closing loops drive retention.
  • In-product feedback keeps development close to real family needs.
  • Founder-led onboarding and success support for the first institutional pilots.

Hub71 edition · Supply side

How we build and defend it

A founder-built platform with governance, partnerships and family insight embedded from day one.

08 · Key partners

Infrastructure and market access

  • Supabase for identity and family data isolation.
  • Railway for hosting and deployment.
  • Stripe for subscriptions and partner billing.
  • Sentry and PostHog for reliability and product analytics.
  • Target partners: ECA, ADEK, Family Care Authority, schools and clubs.
07 · Key activities

Build, learn, distribute

  • Ship product improvements from family feedback and behavioural data.
  • Develop habits, early-money and educational experiences.
  • Measure kept weeks, retention, conversion and revenue per family.
  • Acquire families directly and develop institutional pilots.
  • Operate privacy, compliance, billing and family support.
06 · Key resources

Product, data and founding team

  • Live multi-tenant platform with separate private spaces for each family.
  • Per-child routine, learning and reward history that improves relevance over time.
  • Reusable educational and early-financial-literacy content.
  • Brand, design system and culturally flexible household model.
  • Three Abu Dhabi founders spanning engineering, governance and operations.
Defensibility

The family rhythm becomes the moat

Generic apps hold isolated lists or transactions. Family Hub connects plans, habits, rewards, learning and conversations. As each household builds history, the product becomes more useful and harder to replace without losing its shared rhythm.
