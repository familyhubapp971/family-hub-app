Sign up
Sarah · first contact

Welcome, Pricing, About and Legal pages → "Start free" → your own family web address (like /t/khan-family) → email magic link or Google, no passwords → onboarding wizard: members with roles and kids’ ages, timezone and currency auto-detected, starter habits pre-seeded.

Explore: typos in email, expired magic link, going back mid-wizard, tiny screens.

Dashboard
Sarah + Yusuf · the daily driver

Six tabs: Today, Meals (week planner + snacks), Calendar (repeating events + a private subscribe link that feeds Google/Apple/Outlook), Assignments, Noticeboard (emoji notes), Tasks (assign, filter by member, mark done).

Explore: member filters, week arrows, long titles, empty tabs.

Family
Sarah (admin) + Yusuf · people, roles, safety

Grown-ups (email) and Kids (PIN) in one Manage Family screen: invite adults, add kids without email, set/reset 4-digit PINs, admin toggle (last admin protected), remove members, change your own email safely.

Explore: what a non-admin can and cannot see; invite the same person twice.

Rewards
Sarah (admin) · chores → money skills

Sticker value per family (per-child override), habit pay boosts (2x/3x/5x), optional skip penalty, pocket money. Admin Panel: balances, week history, quick actions (claim, cash out, save, invest, withdraw). Reward shop + kids’ requests.

Explore: change the sticker rate mid-week; can a non-admin reach any of this?

Kid world
Amina + the kids · PIN login

Tap your avatar, enter your PIN (lockout after wrong tries). My World: weekly habit tracker, stickers, weekly account (earned / saved / invested), money skills, reward shop and requests, end-of-week recap.

Explore: role-play an 8-year-old; is anything confusing, unreadable, or reachable that shouldn’t be?

Learn
kids only · Sarah sees insights

Logic (patterns, odd-one-out, if-then, sorting × 3 difficulties), Maths journey (placement, lessons, times tables, challenges, certificates), World Flags (map, flashcards, quizzes, certificates), reading log. Parents get read-only Learning Insights per child.

Explore: quiz exits, certificates, difficulty jumps, quitting mid-lesson.

Child view
Sarah + Yusuf · one child, whole picture

Open any child’s world from the account menu: habits and rewards, meals, calendar, journal, learning insights, and the week-close flow (claim / cash out / save / invest / withdraw with a full audit trail).

Explore: close a week, reopen it, verify the money maths always adds up.

Everywhere
everyone · cross-cutting

Phone-first layouts, finger-sized tap targets, no sideways scrolling, browser back button, double-taps, two adults in the same family at once, the beta feedback widget (bottom corner), sign out and in.

Explore: anything that works on a laptop but not on a phone.
