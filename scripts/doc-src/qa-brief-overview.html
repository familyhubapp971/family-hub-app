★ QA Brief 1 · What you're testing

Break it before
our families do.

Family Hub is a private online space for one family. Parents run the household (calendar, meals, tasks, notices); kids sign in with a PIN to earn stickers for good habits, then save, spend or invest them — chores that quietly teach money skills — plus built-in learning games. Private per family, built for real households: big, blended, twins, grandparents, culturally rooted.

Sarah
The coordinating parent · admin

Runs the family's mental load from her phone. Sets up the family, invites people, configures the reward economy. If anything takes more than a few taps between school runs, that's a finding.

Yusuf
The second adult · invited by email

Uses the everyday screens (tasks, calendar, meals) but must never see admin settings. Tech-fluent; notices rough edges.

Amina
The teen · PIN sign-in

Runs her own habits and money. Too old for anything that feels babyish.

The kids
Ages 5-11 · PIN on a shared tablet or parent's phone

Everything they see must be tappable, readable and safe. Parent settings must be unreachable. Not in this beta: community admins (schools/clubs) and billing — everything is free on staging, no card anywhere.

Your missionthree kinds of gaps
Usability

Where would Sarah get stuck, hesitate or mis-tap? Phone-first (375px; most users are on phones), then tablet, then laptop.

Validation

Does the idea land? What feels valuable vs confusing or pointless? Would you honestly pay $7.99/month as a parent?

Functional

Broken flows, dead ends, wrong money maths, empty states, double-taps, back button, expired sign-in links, wrong PINs.

Reportingone line per finding

Screen → what you did → what you expected → what happened, plus a screenshot and device/browser. Severity: blocker / annoying / polish. Shared doc or sheet; we triage together weekly.
