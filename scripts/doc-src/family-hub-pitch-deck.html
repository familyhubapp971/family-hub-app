Family Hub 2 / 7

Five everyday struggles. Five gentle fixes.

Every feature solves a real problem most parents already live with.

Habits

The problem

Kids need structure. Parents are tired of nagging.

The solution

Stickers, streaks and charts that turn routines into a daily game.

The value

Kids build routines they're proud of. You stop being the bad cop.

Money

The problem

Kids reach 18 without a basic grasp of money or how to manage it.

The solution

Pocket money kids earn: then save, spend, or invest.

The value

Iman knows what it means to invest at five. So can your child.

Learning

The problem

School emails, group chats, notes in bags: things slip.

The solution

Shared calendar, noticeboard, and Learn for daily practice.

The value

You never miss sports day or parents' evening. Learning feels fun.

Heart

The problem

Most days end with “How was school?” “Fine.” Nothing more.

The solution

A daily journal and gratitude prompt the whole family fills in.

The value

Conversations open up. Kids feel seen. So do parents.

Together

The problem

Meals, calendars, plans: all in different heads and apps.

The solution

One shared home view: meals, calendars, announcements, more.

The value

Mental load actually gets shared. Less chaos. More us.

Impact

It's bigger than one family.

Family Hub creates measurable impact across three dimensions.

Social

  • Kids feel seen through daily gratitude.
  • Parents share the mental load.
  • Money becomes a first language at home. A skill that opens every door.
  • Family bonds in a fragmented world.

Economic

  • Replaces 5+ apps families pay for.
  • Saves hours of coordination every week.
  • Kids who save become adults who invest.
  • Stronger financial foundations for life.

Environmental

  • Cuts paper waste from charts and notes.
  • Habit framework scales to sustainability.
  • Tidier homes. Less waste. Less stuff.
