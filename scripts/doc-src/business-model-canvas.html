★ FamilyHub is an all-in-one family platform helping modern households build stronger routines, healthier digital habits, and long-term financial resilience. The platform combines family coordination, beneficial screen time, and embedded financial literacy through a gamified reward economy where children learn to earn · save · invest · spend through everyday family life. Designed to be inclusive, built around the needs of households historically underserved by family-tech, with internationally-relevant experiences such as the Ramadan journal.
