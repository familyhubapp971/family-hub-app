Business Model Canvas

An all-in-one platform for modern family life

FamilyHub is an all-in-one family platform helping modern households build stronger routines, healthier digital habits, and long-term financial resilience.

02 · Value Propositions
  • Family admin: calendars, meals, announcements, assignments, journal.
  • Beneficial screen time: Maths, World Flags, Logic. Kids earn by learning.
  • Sticker economy: earn · save · invest · spend. Financial literacy from early childhood, baked into family life.
  • Cultural flexibility: optional traditions and journals, flexible calendars, extended-family roles. Works for any culture, or none.

03 · Channels
  • Search and content (e.g. “family planner app”, “homeschool app”, “chore rewards app”); Product Hunt launch
  • Paid social campaigns on Meta and TikTok, focused on UAE / Saudi / UK
  • Community partnerships (schools, co-ops, community groups)
  • Mobile distribution: web app today, native iOS and Android upcoming; direct enterprise sales beyond 100 paid families
  • In-app referrals: invite-a-family, both households receive a free month
  • Branded partner portals double as consumer surfaces
01 · Customer Segments
  • Core consumer: tech-comfortable parents with 2+ children, currently juggling multiple apps
  • Underserved consumer: multicultural, multi-generational, blended, and large households
  • Organisations (branded deployments): schools, homeschool co-ops, clubs, NGOs, government-affiliated family programmes (20 to 500+ families)

Launch geography. 1. UAE / Saudi Arabia. 2. US / Canada / UK. 3. Indonesia / Pakistan.

04 · Customer Relationships
  • Self-serve onboarding: under 5 minutes
  • Lifecycle email programme: welcome, day-3 prompt, day-14 progress recap, monthly streak report
  • Retention loops: sticker streaks, weekly meal prompts, child learning progress
  • Enterprise customer success: hands-on until our first five paid organisations
  • Product-led growth: in-app behavioural cues convert free families to paid

How the money works

Cost structure and revenue streams at a glance

09 · Cost Structure
Line Type Notes
Cloud hosting Variable ~$20/mo today, scales linearly with paid families
Identity and database Variable Free tier today, paid tier at scale
Payment processing fees Variable 2.9% + 30¢, built into pricing
AI personalisation Variable Target < $0.50 per child per month
Payroll Fixed Founder team, plus first hire at 100 paid families
Marketing Variable Cost to acquire a family stays under 6× monthly subscription revenue
Compliance and legal Fixed EU, US, and UK child-data regulations
Domain and certificates Fixed Custom domain and wildcard SSL

Cost moat. The cost of serving an additional family is near zero; per-family economics improve with every paid family.
