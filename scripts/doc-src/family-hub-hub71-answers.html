Q2What are you building and how is it different than what is already out there?

Family Hub is the operating system for modern family life: a private workspace per family combining four things no competitor offers together. First, family admin: shared calendar (syncs to Google, Apple and Outlook), meals, tasks, assignments and announcements. Second, a kid economy: children earn stickers by completing habits, converting to pocket money at a rate each family sets. Parents can boost payouts and invest a child's stickers into a habit with a growth multiplier, teaching saving and compounding through chores. No debit card needed, so it works for young kids and cautious parents. Third, beneficial screen time: kid-only learning games (logic, maths, world flags) with parents seeing progress insights. Fourth, cultural flexibility: optional cultural lenses and flexible calendars, built for Muslim and multicultural households the market ignores. Competitors each cover one corner: Cozi and FamilyWall do admin only; GoHenry and RoosterMoney do money only and require a card; Khan Academy does learning only. None model real families: twins, blended homes, multi-generational households, kids logging in with a PIN instead of email. Each family is an isolated tenant, which also lets schools, clubs and community groups run white-label versions.

Q3What is the 'Why Now' for your business?

Three shifts make this the moment. First, the coordination load on parents has never been higher. Dual-income households and the now-mainstream "mental load" conversation have families duct-taping WhatsApp, Google Calendar and paper chore charts. None of it teaches kids anything. Second, screen-time guilt has hit a tipping point. Parents are actively seeking structured, beneficial screen time instead of bans. A product where a child's screen time goes into habits, savings and learning games meets that demand directly. Third, AI-native development changed the economics. Family Hub shipped a production-grade multi-tenant platform at a fraction of traditional cost, so a lean team can profitably serve families the big players ignored. Why Abu Dhabi and MENA: the Gulf is our launch market by design, not adaptation. Families are larger, and faith and family culture are first-class features in our product (Hijri calendar, Ramadan routines), not an afterthought. The UAE is actively investing in childhood financial literacy and family wellbeing, which is exactly what our sticker economy delivers. The product is built in-house, led by me as founder. This model has produced 235 releases in the last 90 days at near-zero engineering payroll.
