Q1 Describe in 280 characters what problem you are solving and how

Family life is split across apps and one parent's head, so mental load grows while routines and meaningful moments slip. Family Hub brings plans, habits, money and connection into one private space, giving children structure and keeping families in each other's worlds.

Q2 What are you building and how is it different than what is already out there?

One private space that turns family routines into shared rituals.

Family Hub replaces disconnected calendars, chore charts, pocket-money tools and learning apps. Parents share meals, schedules, tasks and notices, while children enter through a simple PIN with no email or phone required.

The difference is how everything connects. A child completes a habit, earns stickers and builds a streak. Stickers convert into pocket money at a rate set by the parent, teaching earning, saving and compounding without a debit card. Shared journals and gratitude prompts open conversations. Educational games make screen time useful.

Flexible roles and calendars support blended, multigenerational and multicultural homes, while every family's data remains private. The same multi-tenant architecture lets schools and clubs offer Family Hub under their own name.

Other products solve one family task. Family Hub combines structure, shared mental load, positive habits, early financial learning and daily connection in one weekly rhythm. The result is less chaos, more capable children and a family that stays in each other's world.

Q3 What is the 'Why Now' for your business?

Three shifts make this the right moment.

First, family mental load and screen-time guilt are now mainstream concerns. We interviewed 24 parents; 10 families now use Family Hub at home. They want the same things: shared structure, less conflict around routines, and more meaningful connection.

Second, AI-assisted development now lets a small team build secure consumer software at a cost ordinary families can support. We used that advantage to deliver a live, multi-tenant platform rather than a prototype.

Third, the UAE has made families a national priority through the Year of the Family, the National Family Growth Agenda 2031, and the Ministry of Family. Abu Dhabi also has the schools, family institutions, and financial partners needed to distribute Family Hub at scale.

All three founders are parents based in Abu Dhabi. The need is proven, the product is live, and the local ecosystem is ready. Now is the moment to turn private-beta traction into a regional platform that helps families share the load, build structure and stay connected.

Q5 What success metrics do you measure, and where are you with each of them?

Ten families are using Family Hub in private beta.

We interviewed 24 parents and onboarded 10 families to the live product. They use the full journey at home, from signup and child login to habits, rewards, and learning.

Our current metrics.

We have 10 families onboarded. Across this beta cohort, we are establishing baselines for weekly active families, shared routines completed, parent-closed weeks, family-journal participation and week-4 retention.

Our north-star metric is kept weeks.

A kept week means a family planned together, a child completed their habits, and a parent closed the week and paid the reward. Four consecutive kept weeks show that Family Hub has become part of the household rhythm.

At commercial launch.

We will add free-to-paid conversion, month-3 retention, and revenue per family.

Q13 Who is responsible for building your product?

Family Hub is built in-house by the founding team.

We set product priorities together using our experience as parents and feedback from the families testing the platform. Babatunde leads product and technical implementation, Sandra embeds privacy, regulatory, and governance requirements, and Jumi shapes the operational and partnership needs. Every feature follows written acceptance criteria, review, and automated testing before it reaches a family.

This is not a prototype held together with tape. It is a production platform: every family's data sealed off from every other, sign-in and payments running on proven infrastructure, and a full staging environment so nothing untested touches a real home.

Building in-house keeps product decisions close to the families using it. We can respond quickly to feedback, maintain control over quality, and direct investment towards commercial growth.
