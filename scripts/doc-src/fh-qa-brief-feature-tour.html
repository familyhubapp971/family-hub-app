Sign up
Sarah · first contact
  • Welcome, Pricing, About and Legal pages, then "Start free".
  • Your own family web address, like /t/khan-family.
  • Sign in by email magic link or Google. No passwords.
  • Wizard: members, roles, kids’ ages; timezone and currency auto-detected.
  • First login shows a 4-step Get Started guide: add kids, set PINs, choose sticker value, pick first habits.

Explore: email typos, expired magic link, going back mid-wizard, tiny screens.

  • Meals: week planner plus snacks. Tasks: assign, filter by member, mark done.
  • Calendar: repeating events, plus a private link that feeds Google, Apple or Outlook.

Explore: member filters, week arrows, long titles, empty tabs.

Family
Sarah (admin) + Yusuf · people and roles
  • One screen: Grown-ups (email sign-in) and Kids (PIN sign-in).
  • Invite adults; add kids without email; set or reset 4-digit PINs.
  • Admin toggle (last admin protected); remove members; change your email safely.
  • Built for real families: twins, blended families, grandparents as adults or guests.

Explore: what a non-admin can and cannot see; invite the same person twice.

Rewards
Sarah (admin) · chores to money skills
  • Settings: sticker value per family (per-child override), pay boosts (2x/3x/5x), optional skip penalty, pocket money.
  • Invest and grow: invest a child’s stickers into a habit with a growth multiplier.
  • Kids money (profile menu): one page per child, week-by-week history, and five actions that really save: claim, cash out, bank, invest, take part back.
  • Who can do what: any grown-up moves money (it can all be undone); only an admin closes a week, sets a balance, or changes the family. Doors you cannot use are hidden.
  • Reward shop items and kids’ requests.
  • My World board money row: savings split (earned last week vs kept), investment worth and at-risk count, this week’s progress bar.
  • Investment tags: every invested habit shows at risk or safe plus what it pays; investments list one line each, at-risk first, tap one to open it to see what went in, what rolled over, and what it is worth today.
  • Money follows the family’s currency everywhere on the My World board: change it in settings and check the amounts are written the way that currency is written.
  • Step back to a finished week: it says it cannot be changed, and a Final summary shows what was earned, where it went, and how much of the week got done.
  • Family settings (profile menu): family name, the app-wide currency, export your data, delete the hub behind a typed confirmation. Admin only.

Explore: change the sticker rate mid-week; can a non-admin reach any of this? Do the two savings lines always add up to the sticker total?

Kid world
Amina + the kids · PIN login
  • Tap your avatar, enter your PIN. Wrong-try lockout protects the account.
  • Kids get six tabs of their own: My World, Meals, Calendar, Journal, Learn, Tasks.
  • My World: weekly habit tracker, stickers, weekly account (earned / saved / invested), money skills, end-of-week recap.
  • Reward shop: kids pick a reward and send a request; parents approve and redeem it.
  • Tasks: their own tickable task list.

Explore: role-play an 8-year-old; is anything confusing, unreadable, or reachable that should not be?

Learn
Kids only · Sarah sees insights
  • Logic: patterns, odd-one-out, if-then, sorting; three difficulties.
  • Maths journey: placement test, lessons, times tables, challenges, certificates.
  • World Flags: explore the map, flashcard path, quizzes, certificates.
  • Art: a free drawing canvas. Plus a reading log.
  • Parents get a read-only Learning Insights tab per child.

Explore: quiz exits, certificates, difficulty jumps, quitting mid-lesson.
