★ FamilyHub is the operating system for modern family life. Four pillars no single competitor combines: family admin (calendars, meals, assignments), beneficial screen time (children earn stickers by learning), a sticker economy in which children earn · save · invest · spend from early childhood — financial literacy embedded in everyday family life, and cultural flexibility (Ramadan journal, mosque integration, extended-family roles). Designed for households historically underserved by mainstream family-tech.
